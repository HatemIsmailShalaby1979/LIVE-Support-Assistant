Browser prototype / Snapshot 2026-08-27

A small support tool with explainable behaviour.

A static browser prototype that matches viewer questions against public TikTok LIVE help content and returns a relevant policy with a ready-to-send response.

Where it stands: a portfolio prototype. Five policy entries, keyword matching, no backend, public content only. Not a production support system. No ticket IDs, internal SOPs, or private customer data. No external audit and no revenue.

What this project demonstrates

Simple by design

Keyword matching keeps the result inspectable and easy to explain.

Privacy boundary

No ticket IDs, internal SOPs, or proprietary customer information.

Operational lesson

Start with the smallest useful workflow before adding complexity.

Honest boundary

The assistant matches five policy entries and nothing more. It does not use a language model, does not handle account-specific cases, and is not connected to any ticketing system. Its answers can go stale because the policy text is copied by hand from public help-centre pages.

The founder's story

I spent twenty-eight years in contact-centre operations and workforce management. Forecasting, scheduling, adherence, service levels, churn. The same problems appeared in every company I worked in, and none of the tools solved them properly.

In April 2026 I left that career and started building full time, alone, teaching myself to write software as I went. The first four tools were published six weeks later, in May and June 2026. Each one took a single operational problem and solved it properly. They were not impressive. They were correct.

Those four tools converged into one idea: Helix Codex, an accountable AI operating organization. Not an autonomous agent. An organization with a constitution, named roles with bounded authority, evidence trails, and a human at every consequential boundary. Helix Prime is its operations core.

LIVE Support Assistant is a component of Helix Codex. It is maintained by one person, with no team and no funding.
